Your information

Privacy notice

This notice explains what information this portfolio handles when you browse the site, submit an enquiry, or book a call.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Enquiries and project forms

When you submit a form, the information you provide (such as your name, email, phone number, company, budget, timeline, and project details) is sent to Formspree so I can receive and respond to your enquiry. Do not include passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive personal information.

Formspree processes and stores submissions under its own terms and privacy practices. I use enquiry details to communicate with you, assess your project request, and provide requested services. I do not sell enquiry information.

Call bookings

The booking calendar is provided by Cal.com. If you book a call, the information you enter there is handled by Cal.com under its own privacy practices. Please review its notice before submitting booking information.

Browser storage and activity requests

Your theme preference and the homepage’s latest public repository update identifier are saved in local storage. The welcome offer’s dismissed or claimed state is saved in session storage. These values support site functionality and are not used to identify you.

The homepage may request public activity information from GitHub and GitLab. Those services receive requests from your browser and apply their own privacy practices.

External services and links

The site loads resources from providers such as Google Fonts, Adobe Fonts, Font Awesome, Bootstrap, and AOS, and links to services including WhatsApp and Telegram. These services may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser connects to them. Their handling of that information is governed by their respective privacy notices.

Your choices and contact

You can clear browser storage in your browser settings. You can choose not to submit a form or book a call, though that means I may not be able to respond to your request through those channels.

For a question about this notice or an enquiry you have submitted, email graham@grahamspaul.net.ng.
